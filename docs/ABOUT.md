# About Lumen

Lumen is a fast, private, universal search app for Android. A single search field finds your apps, contacts, files, calendar events, notes, and device settings, and answers quick questions such as calculations, unit and currency conversions, and world clock times.

## What Lumen Does

- **Searches your phone.** Apps, app shortcuts, contacts, calendar events, documents, photos, videos, audio, notes, and system settings appear in one ranked list.
- **Answers instantly.** The built-in calculator, unit converter, currency converter, and world clock respond as you type.
- **Asks AI when you want it to.** Type `ai` followed by a question to receive an answer from Google Gemini using your own API key.
- **Keeps notes.** Write quick notes that become searchable immediately.
- **Meets you anywhere.** Open Lumen from the home screen, a home screen widget, the Quick Settings tile, the assistant gesture, or use it as your launcher.

## Design Principles

- **Local first.** Searching your phone happens entirely on your device. Lumen has no servers and no accounts.
- **Fast by default.** Results appear as each source responds, and slow sources never hold back fast ones.
- **Respectful of permissions.** Each search source asks only for the permission it needs, only when you first use it, and can be turned off at any time.
- **Open source.** Lumen is free software licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
